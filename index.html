THE ORIGINAL EXPERIENCE

One form. Your certificate.

Register your details and participation status. The flow emails a certificate to eligible participants.

  1. Open the form and enter your details.
  2. Choose your participation status and submit.
  3. Check your email for the certificate or update.
Try version 1 ↗
↓ Download the Excel template

Microsoft sign-in may be required. Certificates are delivered as HTML attachments.

Need a little more help? +

Version 1: Use an email inbox you can access. Choose the participation status that applies to you, then submit once.

Version 2: Download the template, replace the sample rows, and keep the column headers and ParticipantsTable name unchanged. Enter Yes or No for each person, following the form instructions. Save as .xlsx and upload. Organization access may be required.

Check spam if an email is missing. Delivery depends on the connected Microsoft 365 flow. To get a PDF, open your HTML certificate in a browser and choose Print → Save as PDF.
